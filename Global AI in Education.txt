2. Introduction
Artificial Intelligence is becoming an important part of modern education. AI tools are being used by students, teachers and educational institutions for learning, teaching and other educational activities.
However, AI adoption is not the same in every country or region.
This project analyzes global AI adoption in education using data visualization and interactive dashboards. The project helps identify trends in AI usage, differences between countries, adoption levels and usage of different AI tools.
3. Problem Statement
The main problem addressed by this project is:
To analyze the adoption and usage of Artificial Intelligence in education across different countries and years and present the findings through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

The project aims to understand:
- How AI adoption is changing over time.
- Differences in AI usage between countries.
- AI adoption in rural and urban areas.
- School, student and teacher AI usage.
- Adoption levels of AI.
- Usage of popular AI tools.
4. Objectives
The main objectives of this project are:
1. To collect and analyze data related to AI adoption in education.
2. To clean and prepare the dataset for analysis.
3. To identify important trends and patterns.
4. To create meaningful visualizations using Tableau.
5. To develop interactive dashboards.
6. To create a Tableau Story for presenting the findings.
7. To integrate the Tableau dashboards into a web application.
8. To provide useful insights about global AI adoption in education.
5. Data Collection
The project uses a dataset containing information related to AI adoption and education across different countries and years.
The dataset contains information related to:
- Country
- Year
- Education Index
- Internet Penetration
- Student AI Usage
- Teacher AI Usage
- School AI Adoption
- Rural AI Usage
- Urban AI Usage
- AI Tools
- AI Adoption Level
- Daily AI Usage
The data was used as the main source for creating the Tableau dashboards.
6. Data Preparation and Cleaning
Before creating visualizations, the dataset was prepared for analysis.
The following steps were performed:
Step 1 – Check the dataset
The dataset was examined to understand the available columns, records and data types.
Step 2 – Check missing values
The data was checked for missing or incomplete values.
Step 3 – Check duplicate records
Duplicate or unnecessary records were identified during data preparation.
Step 4 – Check data types
Numerical fields, categorical fields and date/year fields were checked so that they could be correctly used in Tableau.
Step 5 – Prepare data for visualization
The required fields were selected and organized so they could be used for charts, maps, KPIs and comparisons.

8. Dashboard 1
Global AI Adoption in Education Dashboard
Dashboard 1 provides an overall view of global AI adoption.
It contains several important visualizations.
8.1 Key Performance Indicators
The KPI section displays important values such as:
- Average Daily AI Usage
- Global Count
- Education Index
- Gender Gap
- Internet Penetration
- Rural AI Usage
- School AI Adoption
- Student AI Usage
- Teacher AI Usage
- Urban AI Usage
These KPIs provide a quick summary of the dataset.
8.2 Geographical Trends
A world map is used to display the geographical distribution of the data.
This allows users to compare different countries and understand the global distribution of educational and AI-related data.
8.3 Country-wise AI Usage
A bar chart compares average daily AI usage between countries.
Countries such as Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, Germany, India, Nigeria, Pakistan, the United Kingdom and the United States can be compared.
8.4 AI Adoption Journey
The line chart shows the change in AI usage over the years.
The visualization shows an overall increasing trend in AI-related usage from the earlier years towards 2026.
8.5 Comparison of AI Tools
The donut chart compares major AI tools.
The displayed values are approximately:
- ChatGPT – 26.7%
- Microsoft Copilot – 25.1%
- Google Gemini – 24.3%
- Khanmigo – 24.0%
This shows that multiple AI tools are being used in the education sector.
9. Dashboard 2
Global AI Project: Adoption, Usage and Growth
Dashboard 2 provides a more detailed analysis of AI adoption.
9.1 Rural and Urban AI Usage
This visualization shows the growth of AI usage across rural and urban communities over different years.
It helps understand how AI adoption changes over time across different types of communities.
9.2 Country-wise Education Index
The chart compares education-related index values between different countries.
This makes it easier to identify differences in educational development.
9.3 School AI Adoption Across Regions
This visualization compares school AI adoption across different regions.
It provides a visual understanding of how AI adoption is distributed among different regional categories.
9.4 AI Adoption Level Breakdown
This is one of the important visualizations in Dashboard 2.
The dataset is divided into:
Adoption Level	Records
High Adoption	207
Moderate Adoption	473
Low Adoption	680


The Low Adoption category has the highest number of records.
This indicates that there is still considerable scope for increasing AI adoption in educational environments.

12. Key Findings
The major findings from the project are:
Finding 1 – Increasing AI Usage
AI-related usage shows an overall increasing trend over the years.
Finding 2 – Country Differences
AI adoption and usage vary between different countries.
Finding 3 – AI Tool Usage
ChatGPT has the largest share among the four AI tools shown in the dashboard, at approximately 26.7%.
Finding 4 – Low Adoption
The adoption-level analysis shows:
207 High → 473 Moderate → 680 Low
Therefore, low adoption is the largest category in the dataset.
Finding 5 – Scope for Improvement
The large number of low-adoption records indicates that there is significant scope for improving AI adoption and digital readiness in education.
